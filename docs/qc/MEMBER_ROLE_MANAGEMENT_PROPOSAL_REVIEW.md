## Product blocker and routine protocol corrections

### QC-01 — APPROVED finding / OPEN / policy gate

Option A gives any fresh enabled organization ADMIN authority to promote another member and demote another administrator, without Owner authority, dual control, or step-up. Two cooperating members can acquire administrative authority through one existing Admin; two Admins racing to demote each other leave the mutex winner authorized and the loser denied. The surviving-Admin rule prevents zero usable Admins through this writer; it does not prevent administrative takeover, unilateral removal of a peer's administration, or guarantee two independent administrators. These are consequences to accept or defer, not defects to silently solve by inventing Owner rules.

Required closure: explicit user choice A/B, including peer demotion/promotion, no self-change, preservation of separate grants, and the acknowledged limits of recovery/provisioning. B means no writer. Source: `docs/engineering/MEMBER_ROLE_MANAGEMENT_PROPOSAL.md`, Product decision / Proposed rules / Decision record. The proposal correctly leaves this gate open.

## Locking and current authority assessment

**REJECTED finding: “proposal acquires actor first, creating reversed actor/target deadlock.”** It explicitly prohibits that pattern. Required order is organization mutex → scope proof without profile disclosure → distinct global accounts in UUID string/unsigned order → scoped memberships in that order → role ledger. Java UUID signed `compareTo` is not an interchangeable comparator. Across different organizations, reversed actor/target pairs remain safe only if all participating multi-account writers follow that order. Do not add locks for every Admin merely to count the invariant: holding arbitrary extra accounts after the pair would invalidate the ordering argument. The locked enabled acting Admin, who cannot self-demote, already witnesses the surviving-Admin condition.

**APPROVED verification obligation / REQUIRED FUTURE EVIDENCE:** enumerate participating writer lock paths and prove post-wait authorization using current READ COMMITTED state, not a stale repeatable-read snapshot. The organization mutex alone is insufficient for credential reset, which is global and does not take that mutex. Actor/target account locks prevent enable/version races; the membership locks serialize role changes. Recheck membership presence, enabled state, credential version and role after required waits; choose timestamp after waits and keep membership/ledger/event/audit/receipt atomic. Roll back on audit failure and reject overflow before creating a ledger. Use controlled native PostgreSQL barriers for reversed cross-org accounts, reset waiting on an actor, and an already waiting administrative writer denied after demotion. These are future evidence requirements, not observed failures.

Source comparison: `ApprovalGrantManagementService.mutate` already uses org-first sorted account locks and actor-only receipt replay; `NewTeamDraftService.authorize`, `AgentDraftService.authorize`, `MissionCreateService.authorize` use org/account/membership locking. `ApprovalDetailService.authorize` uses the same org-first pattern for decisions. `backend/src/main/java/com/agentos/auth/RecoveryService.java` locks account then reset-token state and increments credential version; `HumanAuthenticationProvider.java` locks account on login. Reviewed reset/login paths do not acquire organization locks afterward; no concrete cycle is established by these paths. This static inspection is not exhaustive deadlock proof.

## Demotion, transport and grant preservation assessment

**REJECTED finding: “demotion must revoke approval grants or sessions to be secure.”** That would replace the proposed policy. `ApprovalDetailService.authorize` accepts ADMIN/MEMBER and `granted` requires an unrevoked unexpired scoped grant; decision/information flows add snapshot/self/scope checks. Demotion must remove administration on subsequent fresh checks while preserving this independent decision authority. Promotion must not create grants or runtime execution. Plan both a denied administrative write and a still-eligible MEMBER decision, and separately verify expiry/revocation/self/snapshot blockers continue to deny.

**REJECTED finding: “SecurityConfig already makes all new GETs ADMIN-only.”** The filter permits organization-member reads; non-decision writes require ADMIN, and exact approval decision transports intentionally admit members. `backend/src/main/java/com/agentos/common/infrastructure/security/SecurityConfig.java` is only an outer gate: every proposed context/history/receipt endpoint needs service-level enabled/version/current ADMIN checks. Its freshness filter does not eliminate the race between transport admission and a waiting mutation. `MemberAccessService` and grant-management read methods authorize within REPEATABLE_READ snapshots; reads serialized before a demotion may finish afterward. The proposal accurately allows earlier snapshots to finish. A new endpoint must not widen history access merely because its route passes the filter.

## Clarified scope and incorporated engineering defaults

- Both ADMIN → MEMBER and MEMBER → ADMIN require a different, enabled, current same-organization human target. Disabled targets may be inspected but cannot be role-edited. No automatic disable, enable, deletion, invitation or membership creation is delivered. `BootstrapAdmin` creates an account only; first-organization-Admin bootstrap is not delivered.
- The fresh enabled locked acting ADMIN, distinct from the target, guarantees at least one enabled Admin under A. Check that witness after waits; do not acquire every Admin global account lock to count the invariant. Account disabling/removal by other mechanisms remains outside this writer's guarantee.
- Freeze revision as nonnegative signed 32-bit integer, reject exhaustion with conflict, and use deterministic event ordering `recorded_at DESC, id DESC` with bounded 20-item pages. Create no ledger on read, denial, conflict or replay. Role revisions/events/receipts stay separate from grant revisions/events/receipts.
- Give role receipts their own domain/version and organization/actor/key uniqueness; compare the canonical fingerprint before mutable target eligibility. Wrong-target GET returns null; malformed/unknown transport never means rollback. A matching receipt is a historical result, never proof of current role.
- Use generic denial before target disclosure for unauthorized actors. For an authorized actor, prove scoped membership before profile access; use generic scoped not-found for absent/foreign targets. Treat revision/current-role mismatch as conflict requiring refresh/re-review; represent self, disabled and same-role eligibility consistently in frozen context blockers.
- Fresh ADMIN authority governs role command and receipt access. ADMIN role never derives approval authority; separate grants and the existing approval checks remain necessary after promotion and preserved after demotion.
- Revalidate on route entry/reuse, focus or visibility restoration and before review/save/recovery/history actions. Gate private DOM while checking. On any unresolved freshness failure, hide private content and disable actions; on observed denial clear it and retain only allowed GET correlation. Discarded navigation correlation does not turn an unknown outcome into failure.
