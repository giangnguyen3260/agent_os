If B is selected, issuer provisioning/recovery and grant/issuer expiry containment must be specified before a writer is exposed; do not implement a hidden A fallback.

Proposed first-delivery bounds, to be accepted or edited with that choice:

- New grants: only `PUBLISH_PREVIEW`, exact organization + one Team + one recipient + one risk `LOW|MEDIUM|HIGH`. No wildcard/org-wide/inherited grant, no new `CRITICAL`, unsupported action or Agent/tool authority.
- Recipient may be MEMBER or a different ADMIN, currently enabled and a current member of this organization. Reject self, foreign/missing/removed/disabled recipient when issuing. Existing self-decision checks remain mandatory even for a correctly issued grant.
- Duration must be explicitly selected as an integer1..168 hours (maximum7 days), no preselected authority/recipient/Team/risk. Server calculates expiry from the locked write time. No perpetual grant, silent renewal or in-place scope/expiry edit. Renewal is a new reviewed issuance after the old grant expires or is revoked.
- One unrevoked, unexpired grant per exact recipient/Team/action/risk through this writer. A duplicate overlapping live scope returns conflict; expired/revoked historical rows are retained. Do not rewrite or delete legacy rows to force this invariant retroactively.
- Team must be in the same organization and not ARCHIVED. DRAFT is configuration only; a grant never activates a Team, Agent, Task or runtime. Existing decision preconditions continue to decide eligibility.
- Fresh organization authority may revoke self-held, expired, disabled-recipient and legacy/unsupported/CRITICAL grants to reduce authority. A current recipient membership is not required to revoke a known organization-scoped historical grant. Grant ID + organization + recipient binding must be checked before looking up/displaying historical recipient information.
- Revocation is permanent: stamp revoked_at, immutable event and receipt atomically; no delete/unrevoke. Fresh new-key revoke of an already revoked grant is409; exact original receipt replay remains200. Revocation does not undo historical decisions or executions; no executor exists in this slice.
- Required plain-text reason1..1000 Unicode code points for both operations; Java-compatible edge strip, explicit wire normalization, no HTML/link/upload. Reason must contain no password, token or provider credential. Reason belongs to restricted management history, not generic activity/audit payload. Audit records only safe IDs/operation/time.

## Proposed persistence and locking

Additive migration only; preserve V001–V012 checksums and legacy grants. No production seed. Candidate additions: per-organization/recipient grant-ledger revisions; immutable management events containing restricted reason and actor snapshot; durable actor/org/key receipts pointing to the recorded event/result. Provenance must be nullable or absent for legacy rows and honestly labelled unavailable. Grant identity/scope/expiry immutable by this API; only revoked_at can transition null→time. No rollback/delete of historical decisions, receipts, events or grants.

Writes lock the organization first and establish actor/target scope without disclosing foreign account information. New issuance needs two global human-account rows: lock the deduplicated actor+recipient accounts in UUID order, then same-org memberships in deterministic order, then ledger/Team/grant rows. Revalidate fresh credentials/roles/recipient eligibility after all waits. Do not lock actor first and then an arbitrarily ordered recipient: two admins acting across different organizations could otherwise deadlock on shared accounts. Existing ApprovalDetailService must retain organization-first ordering; its single actor-account lock and login/reset single-account locks remain compatible. A receipt-only replay can use the actor-only lock path after the organization lock, but must not acquire actor first and then switch to a new-write two-account path. No cross-organization grant lock or unproven foreign recipient lookup before authorization. Check fresh credential/version/role, target state, expiry and duplicate scope after waits with wall clock. New grant/ledger/event/safe audit/receipt commit atomically; injected failure rolls all back. Receipt replay checks current writer authority but returns the original operation/expiry after mutable Team/recipient/grant changes. Exact immutable fingerprint includes normalized command, expected revision, target and operation; do not fingerprint new current expiry on retry.

Revocation must serialize with existing decision/information writes. A decision that obtains its authorization lock after revocation commits must fail; a transaction legitimately serialized before revocation may commit first. Current read-only repeatable-read inventory can linearize before a later revoke; do not promise retroactive cancellation or immediate clearing of every remote tab. Existing decision receipt replay still requires a currently live decision grant. A management receipt is never decision authority or an execution token.

Same-organization write serialization matches the current baseline; no high-throughput/production-scale claim. A future executor must separately enforce grant revocation/current validity at its execution boundary. Issuing/revoking grants must never call a provider, Tool Gateway, worker or Task/Mission state transition.

## Routine protocol details resolved for candidate A

This section supersedes less-specific earlier DTO/locking descriptions. It is an engineering proposal contingent on selecting A, not a deployed contract. Selecting B requires a separate complete issuer/revoker/provisioning contract and must not reuse A authorization implicitly.

### Exact operation result and receipt rules

POST and receipt GET return the same immutable allowlist: `{operation,organizationId,accountId,actorId,actorName,idempotencyKey,grantId,eventId,revision,recordedAt,expiresAt,revokedAt}`. IDs are UUID strings; operation ISSUE|REVOKE; revision integer1..2147483647; times UTC microsecond timestamps; revokedAt explicitly null for ISSUE and equal to recordedAt for REVOKE; expiresAt always the recorded grant expiry. actorName is the recorded event actor snapshot. No reason/email/private command in this result. FE must match actor, org, account, operation, key and recorded grant/event/revision, not just a response status or current grant list.

Receipt GET requires `?operation=ISSUE|REVOKE`. Under current actor/org issuer authority, lookup by actor+org+key and return null for absent or wrong account/operation without disclosing a different target's result. Exact authorized POST replay compares the stored normalized fingerprint before mutable target/Team/expiry/revision checks and returns the original result. A reused POST key with any different account/operation/body is409. Management receipts do not bypass fresh current actor credentials/membership/role. Revocation/expiry of the issued target grant does not erase authorized historical management receipt recovery; decision receipt replay separately continues to require a live decision grant.

### Revisions, precision and fingerprints

A missing ledger reads revision0, including legacy-only recipients; no read creates rows. Under the organization mutex, a valid first mutation may atomically create its `(organizationId,accountId,revision=0)` row, then compare expectedRevision and increment once with the grant/event/audit/receipt. Successful ISSUE or REVOKE increments exactly1; replay, denial, invalid input, conflict, expiry observed in GET and failed transactions do not increment or leave a new ledger row. Range0..2147483647; refuse a fresh write at the maximum rather than overflowing. Ledger/history survive removal of membership; no membership-cascade delete.

Persist and serialize command-result times at microsecond precision. Capture operation time after all locks and final fresh checks; expiry is that exact instant plus integer durationHours hours. Expired at equality. Duplicate detection queries all legacy/new exact-scope rows where revoked_at IS NULL and expires_at>the same post-wait time. Enforce under the shared organization lock, not an invalid volatile partial-index predicate. The invariant is enforced for API writers using this protocol, not arbitrary DBA SQL.

Canonical fingerprints are SHA256 of UTF-8 JSON with lexicographically sorted object keys, decoded string values, UUIDs normalized to lowercase, JSON integers/booleans/null (not numeric strings), no Unicode NFC/NFKC transform, and canonical timestamps fixed six fractional digits ending Z. A version1/domain `APPROVAL_GRANT_MANAGEMENT` field separates this command protocol. ISSUE command includes org/account/operation/expectedRevision/teamId/actionType/riskLevel/durationHours/reason/acknowledgement. REVOKE includes org/account/grantId/operation/expectedRevision/grantFingerprint/reason/acknowledgement. Fingerprint is stable across retry; do not insert a newly calculated expiry into the command fingerprint. Grant fingerprint binds id/org/account/team/action/risk/expiresAt/revokedAt, including explicit null, using the same canonical encoding. No current mutable profile name belongs in it.

Strict JSON allowlists reject unknown and duplicate fields, omitted required values, explicit null for required fields, numeric strings/fractional integers, coercion, false acknowledgement, malformed UUID/digest or bounds violations. SHA256 is lower-hex64. Reason uses Java String.strip edge whitespace, explicitly mirrored in FE as in Member inventory; length1..1000 Unicode code points after stripping; no other whitespace/line-ending normalization. Allow internal tab/LF/CR for text; reject other U+0000..001F controls and U+007F. Unknown/duplicate command fields are rejected before deserialization can erase them. Duration is integer1..168, expectedRevision integer0..2147483647, action fixedPUBLISH_PREVIEW, risk LOW|MEDIUM|HIGH. Java/JS fingerprint and Unicode/precision conformance oracles are required before exposing the writer.

### Scoped preflight and restricted history

Management context must use explicit allowlists for organization/account, checkedAt, revision, current recipient state and selected policy bounds. No unbounded Team/grant arrays: reuse the existing paginated Team directory for selection; an optional exact selected Team/action/risk tuple in review preflight returns that scope's recorded unexpired count and blockers. Verify the returned selected tuple before acknowledgement. Grant revocation context proves org/account/grant binding before any historical recipient lookup. A removed recipient needs only accountId/current display name/availability and membershipPresent; email need not be disclosed. Treat today's recipient profile as current metadata, not an issuer-name snapshot.

Add proposed GET `/{grant}/management-history?page=0`, fresh current organization ADMIN for A, exact proven grant/org/account binding and fixed20 entries ordered recordedAt,id. Each event allows only `{id,operation,actorId,actorName,reason,recordedAt,revision}`. Only this restricted management surface reads reasons; generic inventory/activity/audit/receipt must not expose them. A future history panel on the scoped grant context uses this endpoint. Legacy grants have empty management history and unavailable issuer/reason/provenance, never fabricated from today's actor. Full preflight DTOs will be code-reviewed against these allowlists before implementation; this proposal is not a claim that these endpoints already exist.

### Revocation and uncertainty limits

Revocation removes **this grant ID only**. Legacy duplicates can leave another matching unrevoked/unexpired grant. Refresh recorded same-scope counts and explain that another record may remain; never label a one-row revoke “remove all approval rights.” Bulk revocation is not part of this command. Existing decision eligibility still accepts any qualifying exact live grant. Revocation blocks later grant-dependent decisions/questions, not an independently valid requester answer.

Within the same captured account, an ambiguous write keeps its exact frozen body/key for manual identical retry until a denial requires erasing the body/reason/private DTOs. After denial, retain only opaque correlation and uncertainty warning; disable POST retry and use fresh authorized receipt GET only. Null remains unknown. Account change erases all previous-account correlation too. No browser persistence of this state. Therefore reload/closing/abandoning the page can lose the exact key and automated recovery; show this limitation before navigation. The committed event/grant still exists in the database and can be inspected through the scoped inventory/history, but similar text/counts/history must not be treated as proof of the exact abandoned operation. Do not claim failure or automatically replace an uncertain write.

Removing membership/disabling an account currently blocks new approvals through fresh checks; restoring membership/enabling before expiry can make an unrevoked grant usable again. This proposal preserves that existing lifecycle behavior and makes it explicit. Permanent cancellation on removal/disablement or credential/membership generation binding would be a separate specified mutation rule; do not silently promise it. Policy A's reciprocal administrator granting and the proposed7-day/HIGH ceiling remain visible product decisions, not engineering defaults secretly promoted to authorization.
