V015 adds organization invitations, durable mail jobs, restricted invitation events/management receipts and a global email-digest mutex table. It preserves V001–V014, existing accounts, memberships, grant/role ledgers and sessions. No production seed; never apply scripts/member-invitations-ci-fixture.sql outside disposable QC. Back up organization memberships, role revisions, invitation provenance/receipts and existing grants before forward migration; do not reset history or use destructive down migrations.

This first implementation invites organization MEMBER only. It does not assign Team/Agent membership, Admin role or approval grants. Existing membership and role are preserved on acceptance. Adding a membership advances the retained member_role_ledgers revision (0→1 on first join; r→r+1 on rejoin), failing atomically at maximum revision. A join is recorded in invitation provenance, not a fabricated V014 role-change event. Retained still-eligible grants may become usable again when an existing member rejoins; current grant, scope, self-decision and snapshot checks continue to apply.

Invitations are disabled by default in application configuration. Local Compose explicitly enables them and routes SMTP only to its non-relaying Mailpit container. Configure AGENTOS_INVITATIONS_ENABLED, AGENTOS_INVITATIONS_FRONTEND_BASE_URL and AGENTOS_INVITATIONS_FROM. Match the frontend URL to APP_PORT, using trusted HTTPS except explicit loopback development HTTP. Inbox URL is http://localhost:8025 with default MAILPIT_PORT. Changing APP_PORT requires updating both invitation and password-recovery frontend base URLs. Do not expose the local inbox publicly or paste its links into logs/tickets.

Durable jobs contain references/state, never a raw token or message body. The sender generates32 random bytes in memory and stores only SHA256 after SMTP acknowledgement and DB commit. A failed replacement preserves the previous committed digest; resend reauthorizes the outstanding invitation under the acting Admin and preserves its original72h deadline. SMTP accepted does not prove human delivery or exactly-once mail. A crash after SMTP ACK and before commit can leave an emailed unusable link. Reopening a valid email link is required after the browser erases its fragment and the page is reloaded.

Organization-first locks serialize lifecycle writers. Acceptance acquires a global recipient-email digest lock before globally sorted existing human rows, rechecks latest issuer/session after waits and adds account/membership/provenance atomically. Unique account email remains the final defense against unsupported concurrent creators; conflict rolls the whole transaction back and requires fresh inspect/login. Existing accounts must sign in and cannot have their password/name/enabled/version/session replaced by an invitation.

An interrupted acceptance may have committed. Its UI must retain UNKNOWN, clear submitted passwords and offer explicit login/workspace verification; a consumed link alone does not prove failure. Management create/resend/revoke can recover through exact actor/org/key receipts; do not issue replacement commands from current status alone. Reverting app code does not undo accepted memberships or erase durable historical receipts. Preserve databases for recovery.

Production SMTP/TLS credentials, abuse/retention controls, KMS/encryption-at-rest policy, production load/failover and broader security review are separate rollout requirements. Passwords are BCrypt hashes and bearer tokens are one-way SHA256 digests; readable restricted recipient email/provenance is not falsely described as one-way encrypted.
